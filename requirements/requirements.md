---

# Features

- **Map view** — opens centered on your location with pins for table tennis, basketball, and boule spots
- **Location detail** — tap a pin to see a description, distance, and availability
- **Navigation** — one tap hands off to your phone's map app with directions
- **Find nearest free spot** — skips browsing and routes you straight to the closest available location
- **Occupancy reporting** — mark a spot as blocked (1 h default); expires automatically
- **Re-route on arrival** — if a spot is taken, instantly find the next free one from the same screen
- **Submit a spot** — add new public locations by dropping a pin, picking a sport type and adding a description
